Privacy policy

DMC at dmc.stjbscouncil.org. Last updated 21 November 2026.

DMC is a multiple-choice practice app run by the STJBS Student Council ("we"). This page says what data the app handles and why. It is short because we keep little.

What stays on your device

DMC works offline and without an account. Your papers, answers, sessions and statistics are stored in your browser on your own device. If you never sign in, none of this leaves your device.

What we collect if you sign in with Google

  • Your Google account email address, name and profile picture, received from Google when you sign in. We use the email to identify your account and to decide whether you can use the school Library.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is secure and HTTP-only, and expires after 30 days of inactivity.
  • If sync is on: your pack outlines, finished practice sessions and results, so you can use more than one device. PDF and image files from your own papers are not synced.

We use Google sign-in only for these purposes. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

School Library

Verified school accounts can post and download practice sets in the Library. Files you post are stored privately and are available only to signed-in Library members. Posting a set means you are allowed to share it with the school.

AI import

If you use AI import, the pages or text you choose are sent through our server to an AI service (NVIDIA) to be turned into questions. Do not import material you are not allowed to share. We do not keep the imported content after the result is returned, other than the questions you save.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Where data is stored and who can see it

Account and sync data is stored with Cloudflare (database and file storage, Asia-Pacific region). Council administrators can see account emails and Library contents so they can run the service. Nightly backups are kept for up to about a month.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time. Clear your browser data to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Ask us to delete your account and synced data by emailing stjbscouncil@gmail.com. We will act within 30 days.

Children

DMC is built for secondary-school students and is used under the school's supervision. Contact us if you believe a student's data should be removed.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date if anything changes. Questions: stjbscouncil@gmail.com.
